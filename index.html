Clinical Manifestation: Signs & Symptoms

Malaria clinical symptoms usually manifest within 10–15 days following an infective mosquito bite.

Early & Uncomplicated Symptoms

  • High Fever & Paroxysms: Cyclical spikes in body temperature (> 38°C / 100.4°F).
  • Shivering Chills: Sudden coldness followed by severe rigor shivering.
  • Profuse Sweating: Sweating episodes as body temperature rapidly falls.
  • Systemic Pain: Severe headache, muscle aches (myalgia), and back pain.
  • Gastrointestinal Distress: Nausea, vomiting, loss of appetite, and diarrhea.

Severe & Life-Threatening Indicators

  • Cerebral Malaria: Confusion, altered consciousness, seizures, or coma.
  • Severe Anemia: Extreme physical weakness, pale conjunctiva, and fatigue.
  • Respiratory Distress: Deep, rapid breathing or pulmonary edema.
  • Jaundice & Dark Urine: Yellowing of eyes/skin and dark/tea-colored urine.
  • Emergency Action: Immediate intravenous or injectable artesunate is required.

Vector Control & Preventive Interventions

Bed Net Barrier (LLINs)

Sleep under Long-Lasting Insecticidal Nets (LLINs) every night, ensuring all sides are tucked securely under the mattress.

Indoor Residual Spraying

Apply WHO-approved Indoor Residual Spraying (IRS) on interior walls to eliminate resting Anopheles mosquitoes.

Environmental Management

Drain stagnant water pools, clear overgrown bushes around habitations, and apply larvicides to breeding grounds.

Chemoprophylaxis & IPTp

Administer Intermittent Preventive Treatment (IPTp) for pregnant women and preventative prophylaxis for travelers.
